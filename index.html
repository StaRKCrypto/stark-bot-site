01 · Perp volume farm

Arcus

What it does

Trades a perpetuals exchange around the clock to build volume for points programs. It aims to quote as a maker, so most fills come from resting orders instead of crossing the spread.

How it works

  1. Reads the market and picks price zones to quote around.
  2. Places resting limit orders on both sides of those zones.
  3. Collects fills, re-quotes, and keeps a kill switch armed.
  4. Reports volume, fills, maker share and points.

FAQ

Questions

Is $STARKBOT live?

No. It is not issued, has no price and no contract. When that changes, the address will be shown in the launch section and on @StaRKCryptoBots.

Where do the stats come from?

From one file, data/stats.json. The current numbers are a one-time copy of a 30-day sample, September 17 to October 17. A script is meant to refresh it later. Missing numbers show a dash.

What does "owner-stated" mean for points?

Points are not read from a chain by this site. They are the numbers the owner reported in the posts, so they are labeled owner-stated.

What does "maker %" mean?

The share of fills that came from resting orders (adding liquidity) instead of orders that took liquidity.

Does this site show balances, PnL or wallets?

No. It never shows wallet addresses, balances, equity or PnL, and it holds no keys.

Is the $500 figure a promise?

No. It is the owner's own estimate of what the points could fetch over the counter. It is not a guarantee and the points may be worth less.

Does the site track me?

No trackers and no third-party requests. The only thing stored is your sound on/off choice, in your browser.

Why does the music wait?

Browsers block sound until you interact. It starts softly after your first tap or key press, and the Sound button mutes it and remembers your choice.
